In the winter of 2023, while touring ski resorts across the West, I had a vision. I wanted to combine two of my greatest passions, mountains and music, into one unforgettable experience. I've always dreamed of bringing a sound system to unique, hard-to-reach places to perform DJ sets and spark spontaneous dance parties. That dream took shape when the idea hit me: mount a sound system to a snowcat.

![Snowcat photo](https://res.cloudinary.com/dctaixj2i/image/upload/v1774993928/img_0613-1_ek8z6o.webp)

It felt like the perfect fusion of terrain and tone.

After researching various vehicles, I discovered what seemed like the ideal fit: the Thiokol Spryte. In a stroke of luck, I found the very same model for sale in my hometown of Missoula, Montana. Previously used by a broadcasting company to access remote, snowed-in mountaintop antennas, the snowcat came with a trailer and a story of its own. In April 2025, I acquired it and began imagining how to turn this machine into a rolling work of art.

![The Spryte](https://res.cloudinary.com/dctaixj2i/image/upload/v1774993918/img_9538-1_nozbc0.webp)

That's when the vision expanded.

What if the snowcat looked like a jackalope? One with flaming antlers, chasing an 8-foot-tall disco ball shaped like a carrot, suspended from a small crane, just out of reach.

![Concept sketch](https://res.cloudinary.com/dctaixj2i/image/upload/v1774993923/img_3189-1_p39dei.webp)

I sketched the concept and reached out to my friend and digital artist, Brenden Hierro. He jumped on board and has since created early 3D renderings to help bring the idea to life.

![3D rendering](https://res.cloudinary.com/dctaixj2i/image/upload/v1774993910/IMG_3195_1_fjjume.webp)
