She's a beautiful little thing, aint she? Our dreams are big, so for them to come to life in a reliable fashion, we have to be sure the engine is running in tip-top shape. We completely re-built the Ford in-line six engine, including replacing the head gasket.

![Engine overview](https://res.cloudinary.com/dctaixj2i/image/upload/v1774994878/PXL_20250903_013600695.MP_syp8cy.webp)

To remove the oil pan, the tracks must come off. This is one of the many things we learned during the engine restoration project.
